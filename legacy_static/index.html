DFA ARCHITECTURE

How Deterministic Finite Automaton Protects Your Order

Unlike typical platforms with arbitrary status updates, every status update is strictly validated against a formal state transition function δ.

1. Mathematical Validation

Transitions such as jumping from "Placed" directly to "Delivered" or modifying terminal states are strictly rejected at the engine level.

2. Immutable Audit Trail

Every state change records the previous state, new state, trigger event, operator, and microsecond timestamp directly in the database.

3. Absorbing Terminal States

Once an order reaches S5 (Delivered) or S6 (Cancelled), it becomes immutable and cannot be tampered with.
